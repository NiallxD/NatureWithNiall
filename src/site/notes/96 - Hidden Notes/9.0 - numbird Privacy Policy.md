# numbird Privacy Policy

# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: March 2026_

numbird is developed by Niall Bell. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and your rights.

---

## What Data Is Used

**📱 Observation Data**
Your eBird data (imported as a CSV/ZIP file) is stored entirely on your device. It is never uploaded to any numbird server. numbird has no servers.

**🔑 eBird API Key**
Your eBird API key is stored locally in your device's UserDefaults. It is sent only to eBird's servers when making API requests on your behalf (e.g. nearby sightings, target species). It is not stored anywhere else.

**📍 Location Data**
When you use live features such as Nearby Sightings or Target Species, your device location is sent directly to the eBird API to return geographically relevant results. numbird itself never stores or receives your location — it goes straight from your device to eBird.

---

## What We Don't Do

**🚫 No Analytics**
numbird contains no analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or usage tracking of any kind.

**📢 No Advertising**
numbird contains no advertising networks or ad-tracking SDKs.

**👤 No Account Required**
numbird does not require you to create an account. No personal information (name, email, etc.) is collected by numbird.

---

## Third-Party Services

**🐦 eBird (Cornell Lab of Ornithology)**
Live features connect directly to the eBird API. Your API key and location coordinates are sent to eBird's servers. eBird's own privacy policy applies to data they receive. See ebird.org for details.

---

## Data Deletion

**🗑️ Deleting Your Data**
You can delete all locally stored observation data at any time from the Dashboard. Uninstalling numbird removes all app data from your device.

---

## Contact

**✉️ Contact**
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact niall@niallbell.com.

---

_This policy applies to the numbird iOS app only._
